# Phase 1 — Learning System & Curriculum Blueprint

This is a content specification, not application code. It is the source material
Phase 2/3 will convert into `lib/data/lessons_data.dart` (or JSON). Nothing here
requires internet, a backend, or AI — every word/sentence below is fixed content
authored once and bundled with the app.

---

## Deliverable 1 — Learning Philosophy

**Who the learner is**
An adult native Kannada speaker who reads Kannada comfortably, understands
spoken Telugu but cannot read it, recognizes a handful of English words
(water, food, tea, yes, no, come, go, good, okay), and has no working
knowledge of English grammar or sentence structure.

**What the app teaches**
Practical, everyday spoken English — the words and sentences she would
actually use at home, at the market, on the phone, or with a doctor. Not
English as an academic subject.

**How it teaches**
Every new idea is introduced through Kannada first, then paired with English,
then reinforced through listening and speaking. Vocabulary is taught before
sentences; sentences are taught as reusable patterns, not one-off phrases to
memorize. Telugu appears only as an optional audio explanation, never as
on-screen text.

**What it intentionally does NOT teach initially**
- Grammar terminology (no "present tense," "auxiliary verb," "article")
- Reading/writing practice in English (this is a listening-and-speaking app)
- Telugu script, or Telugu as a written teaching language
- Idioms, phrasal verbs, formal/written English
- Anything not usable within her actual daily life

## Deliverable 2 — Learning Levels

### Level 1 — Beginner Words
- **Objective:** recognize and recall isolated English words via Kannada meaning.
- **Vocabulary complexity:** single words only (nouns, a few adjectives, numbers).
- **Sentence complexity:** none — vocabulary only.
- **Speaking expectation:** repeat a single word aloud after listening.
- **Approx. lessons:** 15 (Lessons 1–15).

### Level 2 — Simple Sentences
- **Objective:** combine known words into short, useful sentences using fixed patterns.
- **Vocabulary complexity:** words from Level 1 recombined; a few new function words (want, need, have, like, is, this, that, where).
- **Sentence complexity:** 3–5 word sentences, single clause, present tense only.
- **Speaking expectation:** produce a full learned sentence, and substitute one word in a known pattern.
- **Approx. lessons:** 10 (Lessons 16–25).

### Level 3 — Daily Conversations
- **Objective:** use English inside real situations (shopping, phone, doctor, directions).
- **Vocabulary complexity:** situation-specific words layered onto the Level 1–2 base.
- **Sentence complexity:** short question/answer exchanges, 2–3 sentences in sequence.
- **Speaking expectation:** respond appropriately to a simple prompt, not just repeat.
- **Approx. lessons:** 5–8 (Lessons 26–33).

## Deliverable 6 — Sample Lessons (5 fully designed)

Common structure per activity: **Kannada → English → Listen → Choose/Build → Speak**.
Each lesson: 5–8 new words, 0–3 new patterns, ~7 activities, 5–8 minutes.

### Lesson: "I Want Water" (Level 2, Lesson 17)
1. **Objective:** learner can say "I want ___" with 4 known foods/drinks.
2. **New vocabulary:** want, water, tea, food, coffee (5 words — all seen individually in Lessons 9–10, now recombined).
3. **Kannada explanation:** ನನಗೆ ... ಬೇಕು = "I want ...".
4. **Pattern:** I want + thing.
5. **Activity 1 — Listen:** ನೀರು → Water → 🔊
6. **Activity 2 — Multiple choice:** "ನನಗೆ ನೀರು ಬೇಕು" → choose: I want water. / I want food. / I drink water.
7. **Activity 3 — Fill in blank:** I want ___. → water / house / go
8. **Activity 4 — Multiple choice:** "ನನಗೆ ಚಹಾ ಬೇಕು" → I want tea. / I want water. / I like tea.
9. **Activity 5 — Sentence ordering:** [want] [I] [water] → I want water.
10. **Activity 6 — Speaking:** 🎤 "I want water."
11. **Activity 7 — Review:** ನನಗೆ ಕಾಫಿ ಬೇಕು → 🎤 say it in English.
12. **Expected time:** 6 minutes. **Difficulty:** 2.
13. **Remember for later:** "I want ___" pattern reused in Lesson 21 (Give me) and Lesson 27 (Shopping).

### Lesson: "Family" (Level 1, Lesson 5)
1. **Objective:** recognize 6 family words by ear and by Kannada meaning.
2. **New vocabulary:** mother, father, son, daughter, brother, sister.
3. **Kannada:** ಅಮ್ಮ, ಅಪ್ಪ, ಮಗ, ಮಗಳು, ಅಣ್ಣ, ಅಕ್ಕ.
4. **No sentence pattern yet (Level 1).**
5. **Activity 1 — Listen:** ಅಮ್ಮ → Mother → 🔊
6. **Activity 2 — Multiple choice:** Mother → ಅಮ್ಮ / ಅಪ್ಪ / ಮಗ
7. **Activity 3 — Listening:** 🔊 "Father" → which word did you hear? Father / Mother / Brother
8. **Activity 4 — Multiple choice:** ಅಣ್ಣ → Brother / Sister / Son
9. **Activity 5 — Multiple choice:** ಮಗಳು → Daughter / Son / Mother
10. **Activity 6 — Speaking:** 🎤 repeat: "Mother", "Father".
11. **Activity 7 — Review:** mixed recall of all 6 words.
12. **Expected time:** 6 minutes. **Difficulty:** 1.
13. **Remember for later:** these words reappear in Level 3 "At Home" conversations.

### Lesson: "Come / Go / Sit / Stand" (Level 2, Lesson 22)
1. **Objective:** understand and respond to 5 basic commands.
2. **New vocabulary:** come, go, sit, stand, wait (already seen as words in Lesson 13 — now used as commands).
3. **Kannada:** ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಬಾ, ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಹೋಗು, ಕೂಡು, ನಿಲ್ಲು, ಕಾಯಿ.
4. **Patterns:** Come here. / Go there. / Sit down. / Stand up. / Wait.
5. **Activity 1 — Listen:** ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಬಾ → Come here → 🔊
6. **Activity 2 — Multiple choice:** ಕೂಡು → Sit down / Stand up / Come here
7. **Activity 3 — Listening:** 🔊 "Wait" → which word did you hear? Wait / Go / Sit
8. **Activity 4 — Multiple choice:** ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಹೋಗು → Go there / Come here / Wait
9. **Activity 5 — Fill in blank:** ___ down. → Sit / Go / Water
10. **Activity 6 — Speaking:** 🎤 "Come here." / "Sit down."
11. **Activity 7 — Review:** mixed recall of all 5 commands.
12. **Expected time:** 7 minutes. **Difficulty:** 2.
13. **Remember for later:** reused directly in Level 3 conversations (doctor, phone, directions).

### Lesson: "Where is...?" (Level 2, Lesson 24)
1. **Objective:** ask where something is, using 3 known places/objects.
2. **New vocabulary:** where, bathroom (water, home already known).
3. **Kannada:** ... ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?
4. **Pattern:** Where is + thing?
5. **Activity 1 — Listen:** ನೀರು ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? → Where is the water? → 🔊
6. **Activity 2 — Multiple choice:** "ಬಚ್ಚಲುಮನೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?" → Where is the bathroom? / What is this? / Come here.
7. **Activity 3 — Fill in blank:** Where is the ___? → bathroom / go / good
8. **Activity 4 — Sentence ordering:** [is] [where] [water] [the] → Where is the water?
9. **Activity 5 — Multiple choice:** ಮನೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? → Where is the house? / I want water. / Come here.
10. **Activity 6 — Speaking:** 🎤 "Where is the bathroom?"
11. **Activity 7 — Review:** learner asks all 3 "where" questions from memory.
12. **Expected time:** 7 minutes. **Difficulty:** 2.
13. **Remember for later:** core skill for Level 3 "Asking Directions" (Lesson 29).

### Lesson: "Shopping / Vegetable Shop" (Level 3, Lesson 27)
1. **Objective:** handle a short shopping exchange using known patterns.
2. **New vocabulary:** how much, price, two, this (numbers already known from Lesson 6).
3. **Kannada:** ಇದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು?, ಎರಡು ಕೊಡಿ.
4. **Patterns reused:** I want ___. / Give me ___. / How much is this?
5. **Activity 1 — Listen:** ಇದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? → How much is this? → 🔊
6. **Activity 2 — Multiple choice:** How much is this? → ಇದೇನು? / ಇದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? / ಕೊಡು
7. **Activity 3 — Fill in blank:** I want ___ tomatoes. → two / go / water
8. **Activity 4 — Multiple choice:** ಎರಡು ಕೊಡಿ → Give me two. / I want two. / How much is this?
9. **Activity 5 — Sentence ordering:** [is] [how much] [this] → How much is this?
10. **Activity 6 — Speaking:** 🎤 "How much is this?" then 🎤 "Give me two."
11. **Activity 7 — Review (mini conversation):** shopkeeper prompt (audio) → learner picks/speaks the right reply.
12. **Expected time:** 8 minutes. **Difficulty:** 3.
13. **Remember for later:** same exchange shape reused in Doctor Visit and Phone lessons.

## Deliverable 7 — Exercise Types

| Type | Purpose | Example | Difficulty | Introduced |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Listen & recognize** | Connect a sound to a word before reading is required | 🔊 plays "Water" → learner taps the matching Kannada word | 1 | Lesson 1 |
| **Multiple choice (meaning)** | Confirm a Kannada↔English word mapping | ನೀರು → Water / Food / Milk | 1 | Lesson 1 |
| **Multiple choice (sentence)** | Confirm understanding of a full sentence | ನನಗೆ ನೀರು ಬೇಕು → I want water. / I want food. | 2 | Lesson 16 |
| **Fill in the blank** | Practice slotting a word into a known pattern | I want ___. → water / house / go | 2 | Lesson 17 |
| **Sentence ordering** | Build sentence structure sense without grammar terms | [want] [I] [water] → I want water. | 3 | Lesson 17 |
| **Speaking (repeat)** | Practice pronunciation by echoing audio | 🎤 repeat "I want water." | 2 | Lesson 1 (words), Lesson 16 (sentences) |
| **Speaking (recall)** | Produce the sentence from Kannada only, no English shown | ನನಗೆ ಚಹಾ ಬೇಕು → 🎤 say it in English | 4 | Lesson 20+ |
| **Mini conversation** | Respond to an audio prompt inside a realistic exchange | Shopkeeper: "How much?" → learner picks/speaks reply | 5 | Level 3 only |

Exercise difficulty within a lesson should generally follow this order:
listen & recognize → multiple choice → fill in blank → ordering → speaking.

## Deliverable 8 — Repetition Strategy

**Introduction of new words:** 5–8 new words per Level‑1 lesson, 2–4 new
patterns per Level‑2/3 lesson — never more.

**Return schedule (per item, conceptual — Phase 4 implements the scheduler):**
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | First taught, practiced in-lesson |
| Day 1 | Appears again in the next lesson's review activity |
| Day 3 | Appears in a later lesson's multiple-choice distractor or review |
| Day 7 | Appears in a cumulative review activity |
| Day 14 | Appears in a Level‑transition review (end of Level 1, end of Level 2) |

**How difficult words repeat more:** any word answered incorrectly in an
exercise is queued to reappear in the very next lesson's review activity
(Day 0 → Day 1 immediately, skipping ahead), rather than waiting for its
normal slot.

**How sentence patterns are reinforced:** once a pattern is taught (e.g. "I
want ___"), every subsequent lesson that introduces a new noun the learner
could plug into it should reuse the pattern in at least one exercise, instead
of only teaching it once. This is why Lessons 9–10 (food/drink vocabulary)
are taught before Lesson 17 ("I want ___") — the pattern lesson leans on
already-known nouns rather than teaching noun + pattern simultaneously.

This is a conceptual spec — the actual "what to review today" scheduling
logic is a Phase 4 concern (see [main.dart](../lib/main.dart) and
`Progress` model for the fields it will read: `attempts`, `correctAnswers`,
`lastPracticed`).

## Deliverable — Difficulty Progression (5 stages, section 18)

| Stage | Skill | Exercise types used |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recognize words | Listen & recognize |
| 2 | Understand words | Multiple choice (meaning) |
| 3 | Choose correct sentence | Multiple choice (sentence), fill in blank |
| 4 | Build sentence | Sentence ordering |
| 5 | Speak sentence | Speaking (repeat), speaking (recall) |

A lesson's activities should move through these stages in order — never open
with stage 4/5 before the learner has done stage 1/2 for the same content.

## Deliverable 10 — Final Specification

```text
Learner (Kannada-speaking, near-zero English)
        ↓
Kannada explanation (written, primary)
        ↓
English word or sentence (written + audio)
        ↓
Listen (🔊 English; optional 🔊 Kannada/Telugu clarification)
        ↓
Recognition (listen & recognize, multiple choice)
        ↓
Practice (fill in blank, sentence ordering)
        ↓
Sentence (pattern-based construction, reused nouns)
        ↓
Speak (🎤 repeat, then 🎤 recall from Kannada only)
        ↓
Review (Day 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 spaced return; wrong answers re-queued sooner)
        ↓
Real-life conversation (Level 3: shop, phone, doctor, directions)
```

**Success check for every lesson before it ships:** would my mother
understand this Kannada explanation without help? Is the English sentence
one she'd actually say? Does it fit in 5–10 minutes? Are there no more than
5–8 new words or 2–4 new patterns? Is Telugu absent from the screen? If any
answer is no, simplify before adding it to the app.
